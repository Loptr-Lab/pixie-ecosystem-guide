YOUR APP · YOUR CREATIVE WORK

PIXIE walks
with you.

Your assistant producer: helping you understand your producer’s instructions, work through a take, review feedback and return with the thread intact.

You: GarageBand on your iPad or iPhone.
Your producer: Logic on their Mac.
You do not need your own Mac.

Owner-approved direction · 14 November 2026. This page describes the app you want and the gaps to build. It does not record, listen, analyse pitch or control a DAW.

BEFORE AND AFTER ENGAGEMENT

Explore with Johnson. Continue with PIXIE.

Johnson is the pre-hire educational and producer-fit experience. Trying a lesson does not hire a producer, create a contract or imply Ren’s endorsement. After a separately agreed engagement, PIXIE Companion is intended to guide your approved project and producer instructions. Creator OS prepares media for reviewed sharing; Holdings explains and reviews the business journey; Device Stewardship prioritizes repair and reuse. These roles do not imply automatic transfers or completed integrations.

REUSABLE WORK

What we have

Existing code provides a starting point. These pieces have not been verified as a complete assistant-producer workflow.

Project context

Companion has projects, tasks, notes, sources, checkpoints and reviewed imports. They can hold the work and its context.

Creative preparation

Creator OS has local media preview, creative drafts, attribution and reviewed context handoffs. Those messages do not automatically carry audio files.

Draft audio measurements

The Mac-only draft measures loudness, peaks and frequency balance, and processes existing files. Synthetic tests pass. It is not an iPad engine or vocal performance detector.

STILL TO BUILD OR VERIFY

What we don’t have yet

Your assistant producer

An integrated conversation that explains the producer’s directions, guides your next step, records your progress and resumes the recording or revision session. The current helper offers predefined guidance.

Pitch and timing feedback

Vocal pitch tracking and comparison with the intended melody, key and timing, including confidence and limitations. Loudness and spectral measurements cannot establish whether you sang the intended notes.

A tested device workflow

Audio selection and feedback on your iPad/iPhone, verified GarageBand interaction where supported, and a tested exchange with Logic. Recording control, background listening and full editable project round trips are not established.

The current native server’s Claude registration came from the earlier specification. It is not approved PIXIE app onboarding. Removal/replacement and an actual PIXIE connection remain outstanding.

THE JOURNEY TO VALIDATE

Instructions → practice → review → continue

  1. 01Bring the producer’s instructions and the selected reference into your project.
  2. 02PIXIE explains the next step in context while you work in GarageBand.
  3. 03Select a take for supported, evidence-linked feedback. No automatic listening.
  4. 04Make your listening decisions, revise and keep notes connected to the take.
  5. 05Review what to share with your producer and preserve versions when work returns.

Human listening decisions remain authoritative. Real-time feedback versus feedback after a take is still to be specified; this record chooses neither.
